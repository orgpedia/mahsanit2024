The Proposal: -
In order to achieve the target of providing "Har Ghar Nal Se Jal" water supply to rural households by 2024, the Government has decided to implement Jal Jeevan Mission in the State as per the Government Decision No. 1 in the context. According to this government decision, the Jal Jeevan Mission in the district
The entire responsibility of implementation has been entrusted to the District Water and Sanitation Mission.
District Water and Sanitation Missions have been set up in each Zilla Parishad under Section 79 (a) of the Maharashtra Zilla Parishad and Panchayat Samiti Act, 1961, through which Jal Jeevan Mission is being implemented in the districts. Therefore, the role of the Executive Engineer, Rural Water Supply Department in the Zilla Parishad is very important for the effective implementation of Jal Jeevan Mission in the district.
Posts of Executive Engineers in Rural Water Supply Department in Zila Parishads of the State
The executive engineers from the Maharashtra Life Authority are filled by deputation.
Deputation orders are issued by the Maharashtra Life Authority. At present, the posts of Executive Engineer in Rural Water Supply Department are lying vacant in many Zila Parishads in the state.
